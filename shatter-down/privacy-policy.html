Shatter Down Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short

  • Shatter Down has no accounts. The only personal detail it asks for is a player name, which never leaves your device.
  • Your name, progress, items and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, such as your advertising ID, to show and measure ads, as described below.
  • Where the law requires it, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • There are no real-money purchases.

1. Who we are

Shatter Down (the "Game") is made by Shadow Studios ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is collected when you play the Game on Android, why, and the choices you have. If you have any questions, contact us at helpshadowstudios@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The Game saves the following on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • the player name you choose (it is only shown inside the Game)
  • your best score, deepest section and play statistics (such as runs played and rings smashed)
  • in-game items: coins, diamonds, keys and the balls you have unlocked
  • your settings (sound, vibration, visual effects) and how many free rewards you have claimed today

This information stays on your device. We do not receive it, and it is not linked to you. You can delete it by using "Reset progress" in Settings, by clearing the app's data in your Android settings, or by uninstalling the Game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game is free and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. This includes a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad between some games, and optional rewarded ads that you can choose to watch for in-game rewards (such as continuing a run, doubling diamonds, or free coins, diamonds and crates).

To show, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version
  • ad interactions, such as which ads were shown, tapped or watched
  • diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data from the ads SDK

Google uses this information to deliver ads, to limit how often you see the same ad, to measure ad performance, to prevent fraud and, where you allow it, to show ads based on your interests. We do not receive information that identifies you personally from Google. Google's use of this information is governed by its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • How Google uses information in advertising

4. Your consent and choices

  • Consent popup. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where the law requires it, the Game shows Google's consent message the first time you open it. You can accept, decline, or choose which purposes you allow. If you decline personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Changing your choice. Where a choice is required, an "Ad privacy choices" button is shown in the Game's Settings so you can review or change your consent at any time.
  • US state privacy rights. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, such as California, you may have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Where this applies, Google's message in the Game lets you opt out.
  • Advertising ID settings. In your Android settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads), you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You never have to watch a rewarded ad to play the Game.

5. Information we do not collect

We do not run our own servers for the Game, and we do not collect or store your email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or payment details. The Game has no accounts, chat or in-app purchases. In-game crates are earned by playing or by watching optional ads and cannot be bought with real money.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

7. Data retention and security

Information saved on your device stays there until you reset your progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the Game. Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. All communication between the Game and Google's ad services is encrypted in transit.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information. Because we do not hold personal information about you, most requests about ad data are best directed to Google using the links above. You are welcome to contact us at any time and we will help where we can.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes, for example if new features or services are added. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
